LITHOGRAPHY (EXPOSURE · TRACK · METROLOGY)

Exposure, Track & Metrology Tool Repair & Maintenance

29TECH is your single point of contact for parts and PCB repair, refurbishment and maintenance of exposure, track and metrology tools and their peripherals. The work is delivered through our technology partner and cooperating companies, and scope is confirmed for each case.

Exposure tools (steppers / scanners)

  • ASML PAS 5500 series: repair of control boards, wafer handler, stage and illuminator units, and interferometer laser heads
  • ASML PAS 5500 series: PM, repair, installation / relocation, parts supply (scope confirmed per case)
  • ASML TWINSCAN: unit-level repair of encoder boards, alignment lasers, stage parts (chuck clamp, flex cables), reticle library, etc. (scope confirmed per case)
  • Nikon NSR steppers (g-line G7/G8, i-line) and scanners (S series): repair of LS relay amplifiers, alignment and interferometer lasers, CPU and driver boards
  • Canon FPA i-line steppers (e.g. FPA-3000i4/i5) and MPA mirror projection aligners: parts repair, including interferometer laser heads
  • Ultratech steppers (1000–1500 series): supply / repair of servo motors and stage parts; Perkin-Elmer Micralign: parts on request (scope confirmed per case)
  • Refurbished exposure tools on request: Canon FPA series (i-line / KrF), Nikon NSR i-line steppers, etc. (availability, specification and installation scope confirmed per case)

Coater/developer tracks

  • TEL CLEAN TRACK (MARK series to ACT series): parts and PCB repair
  • TEL CLEAN TRACK (MARK, ACT, LITHIUS series), SCREEN (formerly DNS) RF³, etc.: refurbishment / supply of refurbished tools, 150/200/300 mm (6/8/12-inch) (scope confirmed per case)
  • Refurbished tools: modification / upgrade on request (feasibility and scope confirmed per case)

Metrology (CD-SEM · overlay · film thickness)

  • Hitachi CD-SEM (S-8000 / S-9000 series, CG4000, etc.): parts supply, repair, refurbishment (scope confirmed per case)
  • Overlay metrology: parts repair (controller boards, etc.) and refurbishment for KLA-Tencor 5100/5200/5300; laser-scale repair for KLA-Tencor Archer; parts repair for Overlay Q200/Q220/Q230/Q240 (scope confirmed per case)
  • Film-thickness metrology (Nanometrics NanoSpec, Atlas, QS series FTIR; Nova i500): parts repair, refurbishment, modification, PM and troubleshooting
  • Other metrology (Therma-Wave Opti-Probe OP2600 / OP3290 DUV, Rudolph FOCUS / S200 / S300, KLA-Tencor UV series / Prometrix): refurbishment, installation / relocation, calibration, PM

Peripherals & units

  • MKS Baratron capacitance manometers (for lithography, CVD, PVD and etch tools): diagnosis, repair, calibration (calibration report and traceability conditions confirmed per case), periodic maintenance (PM)
  • MKS Baratron 622/623/625–629/631, 722/727/728/740 and E27H/E28H/E29H, etc. (heated / non-heated), and MKS 902 series (piezo): for unrepairable or discontinued units we propose a successor or commercially available alternative model, used only with your approval
  • Peripheral units: repair of Asyst SMIF control boards and fans, transfer robots (Brooks, Rorze, Yaskawa, Kawasaki), chillers, HV power supplies and turbomolecular pumps (scope confirmed per case)

Work is carried out through 29TECH's technology partner and cooperating companies. Scope, lead time and warranty are confirmed for each case and stated in the quotation.
