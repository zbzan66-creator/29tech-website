Vacuum | MKS gauges

Repair and calibration of MKS gauges, plus compatibles

We handle fault diagnosis, repair, calibration and periodic maintenance of MKS capacitance manometers (Baratron®). For units that cannot be repaired or are discontinued, we propose compatible products (not OEM genuine).

16MKS gauge series covered for repair
9MKS models with a compatible product
5Standard F.S. ranges of compatibles (0.1-1000 Torr)

Repair coverage (feasibility confirmed per unit)

  • Baratron®: 622 / 623 / 625 / 626 / 627 / 628 / 629 / 631
  • Baratron®: 722 / 727 / 728 / 740 · E27H / E28H / E29H
  • MKS 902 series
  • Sensors: Standard / Etch Baffle / heated (45, 100, 150, 200 °C) / unheated
  • Repair of capacitance manometers from makers other than MKS can also be discussed (feasibility confirmed case by case)

Services

  • Fault diagnosis: locating faults in circuits, sensors, heater units, etc.
  • Repair: part replacement and repair (type of replacement part confirmed at quotation)
  • Calibration: accuracy check, zero and span adjustment (calibration certificate available)
  • Periodic maintenance: scheduled inspection, cleaning, preventive care
  • Unrepairable or discontinued units: compatible product proposed (not OEM genuine)
  • Models not listed: after checking the unit's specifications (range, fitting, output), we assess case by case whether a compatible product can be proposed

Compatible products: target models (not OEM genuine)

  • MKS models covered: 624H / 625H / 626D / 627H / 628H / 631F / E27H / E28H / DA02
  • For the DA02, a compatible product with EtherCAT output is proposed
  • Other MKS models (622, 623, 722, 727, 728, 740, 902, etc.) are discussed case by case after checking specifications
  • Other makers' models that can be replaced (examples): INFICON CDG045D / CDG025D, Edwards Barocel 600 / 655 / 658 / 659, Setra 750 / 760, Brooks CMC / XacTorr, ANELVA M-341DG / M-342DG

Compatible products: key specifications

  • Metal diaphragm, nickel-based alloy body, fully welded; suited to corrosive gases
  • Full scale: 1000 / 100 / 10 / 1 / 0.1 Torr (intermediate ranges follow the nearest standard range)
  • Accuracy grades: 0.15% / 0.25% / 0.5% of reading (from 0.25% at 100 °C and above)
  • Detailed specifications (resolution, response time, temperature drift, etc.) provided on request
  • Sensor temperature: unheated / 45 °C / 100 °C and others (higher temperatures confirmed case by case)
  • Power ±15 V, output 0–10 V DC, DB15, 2 setpoints
  • Output and communication options (4–20 mA, RS485, DeviceNet, Ethernet, EtherCAT, etc.) on request
  • Automatic temperature compensation; built-in full-scale and zero adjustment (one-touch zero)
  • Fittings: NW16-KF / NW25-KF / 8VCR / 4VCR / 12 mm tube
  • Ceramic-diaphragm type (F.S. 1 to 1000 Torr) also available (delivery format advised per case)

What we need to select a compatible product

  • Maker and model to be replaced; pressure range and required accuracy
  • Mounting position, flange/fitting and orientation; sensor and chamber-wall temperatures
  • Output (analog / digital), power supply (±15 V / 24 V), number of setpoints in use
  • Process gases and by-products; whether the gauge is used for pressure control
  • Compatible products are supplied in specific connector and output configurations; pin assignment (relay, status signals, etc.) is checked per model
  • Cannot be paired with the existing OEM controller (readout)
  • Warranty terms for compatible products are set by individual contract

CMP and vacuum equipment | Repair service

Repair of CMP pneumatic units and vacuum control valves

We repair and function-test MKS-made 4-port / PLUS 4-port UPA pneumatic units used on Applied Materials CMP systems, and repair and overhaul vacuum control valves.

4-PORTCMP UPA (AMAT P/N 0190-15840 etc.)
300 mmFor Reflexion® LK and similar CMP tools
HeLeak check after valve overhaul

CMP UPA (4-port / PLUS 4-port)

  • Target examples: MKS-made 4-port / PLUS 4-port UPA (AMAT P/N 0190-15840 etc.; P/N and revision confirmed from the label)
  • Host tool: 300 mm CMP systems (Reflexion® LK and similar; fit confirmed case by case)
  • UPA (upper pneumatic assembly): pneumatic unit that regulates pressure to the polishing-head zones (multi-zone pressure/flow regulator)
  • Other P/Ns and revisions in the same family are discussed after checking configuration and revision

UPA repair and function test

  • Fault diagnosis and repair (type of replacement part confirmed at quotation)
  • Function test on a test fixture after repair
  • Repair scope and check items are advised per unit, according to its condition

Vacuum control valves

  • Target example: MKS Type 153 control valve
  • Target examples: VAT 65048-PHCG-APJ1 (pendulum valve) / 61238-PAAP-AIM4 (valve controller)
  • Pendulum valve: body cleaning, all O-rings changed (standard FKM; customer-supplied O-rings accepted)
  • Pendulum valve: actuator overhaul (motor / solenoid / plain), bearing replacement
  • Pendulum valve post-overhaul tests: He leak check and function test

Before you send a unit

  • Post-repair service life and warranty are advised per unit, by model and condition
  • If a unit cannot be repaired, we will discuss the next steps with you
  • This service is not provided or authorized by Applied Materials, MKS or VAT

Component-level repair of power, drive and control units

We repair power supplies (including RF), servo and inverter drives, PLCs, HMIs, industrial PCs, control boards and instruments of semiconductor and facility equipment at component level, helping to cut equipment downtime.

Power supplies

  • Power supply units for semiconductor manufacturing equipment
  • RF power supplies (e.g. Advanced Energy units)
  • High-voltage power supplies
  • Switching power supplies, DC power supplies, high-power adjustable DC supplies
  • Power and control boards inside the unit repaired at component level
  • UPS board repair can also be discussed (feasibility confirmed case by case)

Drives and motors

Controls and boards
