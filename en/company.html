TECHNOLOGY PARTNER'S FACTORY

Technology Partner's Factory: Facility & Inspection

29TECH's repair and manufacturing work is carried out mainly at the technology partner's factory. Here are its key equipment and controls.

6,500m²Total floor area
379.75m²Clean room (class 1,000)
843.75m²Temperature-controlled machining area, no copper materials brought in
30 minFirst report on an abnormality

Machining, cleaning and assembly

  • Metal and plastic machining areas are kept separate
  • 5-axis machining center / mill-turn machine
  • Precision engraving machine / vertical machining centers
  • Tank-type cleaning unit
  • Assembly to SOP (work instructions with step, description, standard, illustration, operator signature and date fields)

Measuring, inspection and testing

  • Microscope / 2D image measuring machine / CMM
  • Hardness tester / roughness tester / resistance vacuum gauge, etc.
  • Dynamic balancing machine
  • Vision-based automatic measuring system (introduced to remove operator-dependent measurement variation)

Quality management

  • MES, SPC, MSA and standardized management
  • First-article confirmation flow across sales, engineering, production, quality and purchasing
  • Finished-product inspection flow: full inspection → OQC sampling → stock-in; rejects are reworked or scrapped
  • On an abnormality: report within 30 min → defect report logged in ERP within 24 hrs → related production stopped, impact scope confirmed, customer notified → root-cause investigation, corrective action, effectiveness check, standardization
  • Inspection records are managed centrally and traceable to the form and the operator

Note: Figures are based on the technology partner's data. Equipment counts, models and accuracy are advised individually.
