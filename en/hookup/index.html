Primary & secondary hook-up In preparation

From the fab's supply systems to the tool.

A hook-up business plan for semiconductor fabs in Japan. We aim to take on both primary and secondary hook-up, from the main piping to the tool.

BUSINESS BLUEPRINT

Four steps to a hook-up business

We start by building the team, then expand from secondary to primary hook-up and a one-stop service.

  1. STEP 1

    Building the team

    Partner-company network and training.

    We are planning a team of about 200 people.

  2. STEP 2

    Secondary hook-up

    Tool hook-up: piping and connections from the POU to each tool.

  3. STEP 3

    Primary hook-up

    The fab's main piping and exhaust systems.

  4. STEP 4

    One-stop service

    Parts supply, repair and retrofit together with hook-up.

PRIMARY HOOK-UP

What primary hook-up covers

Main piping from the gas and utility supply systems through the sub-fab to each point of use (POU), and the facility exhaust system.

01

Bulk & specialty gas mains

  • Supply system to main header
  • Header to each point of use (POU)
  • Helium leak testing and purging
02

Utility mains

  • Process cooling water (PCW)
  • Clean dry air (CDA)
  • Nitrogen (N2)
  • Ultrapure water (UPW)
03

Exhaust & abatement systems

  • Exhaust ducts (general, acid, alkali, organic)
  • Scrubber exhaust lines
04

Work management & records

  • Installation drawings and material lists
  • Weld and leak-test records
  • Records submitted to the customer

SECONDARY HOOK-UP

What secondary hook-up covers

From the point of use (POU) to each tool: piping, connections and work records.

01

Process & specialty gas lines

  • SUS EP (electropolished) tubing
  • Orbital welding
  • Fitting connections
  • Helium leak testing and purging
02

Vacuum & exhaust lines

  • Forelines (tool to vacuum pump)
  • Connection to abatement units
  • Heating-jacket installation
03

Utility lines

  • Process cooling water (PCW)
  • Clean dry air (CDA)
  • Nitrogen (N2)
04

Work management & records

  • Installation drawings and material lists
  • Weld records
  • Leak-test records
  • Records submitted to the customer

SCHEMATIC

Primary and secondary hook-up at a glance

Primary hook-up is the main piping from the supply systems to the POU, plus the facility exhaust ducts; secondary hook-up connects the POU to the tool, and the tool to abatement.
