Privacy Policy & Site Terms

How we handle personal information received through inquiries.

1. Business operator

NIKU TECH CO., LTD. (29TECH), Aruba Ginza-dori Bldg., 4-22 Chuogai, Chuo-ku, Kumamoto-shi, Kumamoto 860-0802, Japan. Representative Director: Tao Yingxue.

2. Information we collect

Name, company and department, e-mail address, telephone number and the content of your inquiry, as provided by you.

3. Purposes of use

To answer inquiries; to prepare quotations and proposals and perform contracts; to contact you and send materials; to manage transactions.

4. Provision to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law. If information must be shared with our technology partner outside Japan for a quotation or repair, we will tell you the country and the information concerned in advance and obtain your consent. We will also provide information on that country's system for the protection of personal information and on the measures the recipient takes to protect it.

5. Security

We take necessary and appropriate measures, including access control, to prevent unauthorized access, loss or leakage of personal information. E-mails sent to us are stored on a cloud e-mail service (Gmail) provided by a business operator in a foreign country (the United States). Details of the security measures we take, including how we take account of the legal environment of that foreign country, are provided without delay on request to the contact point in section 7.

6. Requests for disclosure etc.

We respond without delay, in accordance with the law and after verifying your identity, to requests for notification of the purposes of use, disclosure, correction, addition or deletion, suspension of use or erasure, and suspension of provision to third parties. Please send your request by e-mail to the contact point in section 7; we will tell you how your identity is verified when you contact us. No fee is charged for these requests.

7. Contact

NIKU TECH CO., LTD. — e-mail komatsu.nk.tng@gmail.com, telephone +81 90-4230-7434.

8. Revisions

This policy may be revised as necessary; the revised version is published on this page. Established 25 October 2026.
