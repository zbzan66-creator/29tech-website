SCRAP PURCHASING

We buy your plastic scrap
as raw material.

Mainly from factories, trading companies and recyclers around Kumamoto — other regions are welcome to ask. What we buy goes back into the loop as recycled raw material.

What we buy (examples)

  • PE: stretch and packaging film, bottle caps, wheelie bins, fuel tanks, pipe, IBC tanks
  • PP: bulk bags (FIBC), containers and pallets, non-woven fabric, appliance parts
  • Moulding off-cuts, sprues and runners, rejects
  • ABS, PC, PS and other resins — please ask

For a quotation, please tell us

  • Resin and item
  • Volume per month and frequency
  • Packing: bales, bulk bags, flakes or regrind
  • Where it is stored
  • Photos of the material

We buy materials that can be traded as valuables. We do not take on collection, transport or disposal of industrial waste.
