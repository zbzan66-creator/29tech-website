How to order · FAQ

How to request a repair or a part.

From your first inquiry to delivery and warranty, 29TECH in Kumamoto is your single point of contact. What to send, which forms to use and how to ship.

REPAIR FLOW

From request to delivery, in eight steps

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Send the part number, quantity and symptom by e-mail or phone. If the part number is unknown, a photo of the nameplate is enough to start.

  2. 2

    Feasibility reply

    We check whether the part can be repaired and tell you the quotation conditions — for example whether we need to see the unit and where the work will be done.

  3. 3

    Shipping or pick-up

    Ship the unit with the repair request form and the decontamination record. In the Kumamoto area we can also pick it up.

  4. 4

    Incoming inspection

    We check appearance, part number and serial number, and diagnose the fault.

  5. 5

    Quotation and approval

    The quotation states the work, price, lead time, work location and warranty. Work starts after your approval.

  6. 6

    Repair

    Disassembly, cleaning, part replacement and adjustment, following SOPs (work instructions).

  7. 7

    Final inspection and report

    Every unit is inspected and shipped with an inspection report issued by 29TECH.

  8. 8

    Delivery and warranty

    Any fault within the warranty period is investigated and reported in 8D format.

If the part cannot be repaired

We tell you why, and you choose return or disposal. We can also propose a refurbished, compatible or used replacement. How diagnosis and return costs are handled is explained before we start.

If the work is done outside Japan

The work location is stated in the quotation, and your part leaves Japan only after your written approval. Export classification (FEFTA / EAR), export procedures and decontamination checks are handled under 29TECH's responsibility.

REQUEST FOR QUOTATION

What to send for a quotation

The more of this we have, the faster and more accurate our answer. Send what you know.

Parts repair

  • Maker, OEM part number, serial number
  • Tool, chamber and process it is used in
  • Symptom and when it occurs (error codes etc.)
  • Quantity and required date
  • Photos of the nameplate and the faulty area
  • Gases and chemicals it has been in contact with

Compatible parts

  • OEM part number and description
  • Drawing or specification (under NDA), or the part itself / photos
  • Material, dimensions, surface finish and cleanliness requirements
  • Quantity: first evaluation lot and expected annual volume
  • Tool and process it is used in

Used parts and tools

  • Maker, OEM part number or model
  • Quantity and required date
  • Condition you accept: new, unused or used; function check needed or not
  • Tool and application
  • Destination country, if the part leaves Japan
✉ E-mail a request (template included)

SHIPPING

When you send us a part

Documents

Enclose the repair request form and the decontamination record, and e-mail them to us before shipping.

Boards, power supplies, controllers

Put them in antistatic bags and fix them with cushioning.

Vacuum and chamber parts

Close the openings with flanges or caps and double-bag the part in clean bags.

Parts in contact with gas or liquid

Pumps, valves, MFCs etc.: decontaminate and purge them, and drain any liquid.

Heavy units

Pumps, chillers etc.: fix them on a pallet or in a crate. Ask us about transport.

What we cannot accept

Parts that have been in contact with toxic, flammable, corrosive or pyrophoric gases or chemicals and come without a decontamination record.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I ask about a part number that is not listed?

Yes. The catalog shows part of the part numbers we handle. Send us the part number, model or a nameplate photo and we will check.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the part, its condition and where the work is done; the lead time is stated in the quotation (guide value: ESC 20–60 days).

What if the part cannot be repaired?

We tell you why, and you choose return or disposal. We can also propose a refurbished, compatible or used replacement. How diagnosis and return costs are handled is explained in advance.

Is there a warranty?

ESC: 12 months. For other items the warranty is stated in the quotation; its start date and scope are set in the quotation and the basic transaction agreement.

Do you supply an inspection report?

Yes. Every unit is inspected and shipped with an inspection report issued by 29TECH.

Where is the work done?

Mainly at our technology partner's factory. The work location is stated per item in the quotation, and a part leaves Japan only after your written approval.

What do I need to send with a used part?

The repair request form, the decontamination record and safety information such as the SDS. Parts without a decontamination record cannot be accepted.

Is it safe to give you our drawings?

Drawings and specifications are received under an NDA, used only for your order and never disclosed; the same obligation is imposed on our technology partner. We can sign your company's NDA.

Are compatible parts the same as genuine OEM parts?

No. Compatible parts are not genuine OEM parts. They are made to your drawings, specifications or sample and shipped with an inspection report. We recommend starting with a small evaluation lot.

Are you an authorized distributor of the OEMs?

No. 29TECH is not an authorized distributor or authorized repair provider of the OEMs. Part numbers are for fit reference only.

How do I know the condition of a used part?

Stock and condition — new, unused or used, and whether it has been function-checked — are confirmed per part number before you order.

How are invoicing and payment handled?

NIKU TECH CO., LTD. (29TECH, Japanese corporation) invoices in JPY, payable to a domestic bank account. Payment terms follow your company's rules, as agreed.

What documents can you provide for opening an account?

Yes — the company profile, a pledge on exclusion of anti-social forces, supplier survey forms and chemical substance surveys, in your company's format. Registration as a qualified invoice issuer is in progress.

Can fabs outside Japan order?

Our business covers Japan, Korea, Singapore, Taiwan and more. Contract and import / export conditions are agreed case by case.
